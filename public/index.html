Learn the words you actually need. Build your own collections and study them with flashcards, quizzes and spaced repetition, on any device.

Two ways to study

Flash Mode

Tap to turn

See the word and tap the card to turn it. Swipe right if you know it, left if you don't.

Quiz Mode

Tap to turn

Pick the right translation from up to four options. Get it right the first time and the word moves up.

Words come back when it counts

Spaced repetition decides what to study today. Each time you know a word, it waits longer before coming back. Miss it, and it returns a few cards later.
